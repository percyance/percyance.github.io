About Me

I am a Ph.D. student and Graduate Research Associate in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at The Ohio State University (2024–present). My research focuses on brain–computer interfaces (BCI) and neural signal decoding, where I develop deep learning methods for modeling intracranial neural recordings such as local field potentials (LFP).

Before joining OSU, I received my B.Eng. from the School of Electronic Information and Communications at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), and spent a year as a visiting student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at UC Santa Barbara. My earlier work explored ensemble deep learning for medical image classification.
